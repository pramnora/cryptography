BINARY CODES: 1/O...USED TO STORE 'COMPACT' DATA ENTRIES
              USED WITH SOME DATA ENTRY 'KEY'
--------------------------------------------------------
    
Binary codes, can be used to very quickly and easily represent data...;
by simply making use of: 1/0; one might think of it as a 'tick'...;
where: 1 represents did it/and, 0 represents didn't do it. 
  
However, one also needs to keep hold of a, key...;
which explains what each of the separate data entry digits is referring to mean.  

KEY:
  
Went out/Stayed in: 1
Shopped: 1
Online/Offline: 0  
Exercised: 0
-etc.

Personal diary:
1100 (I went out/shopped/offline/didn't exercise)

-etc.
