If we think of a number...let's say for sheer simplicity sake the number, 10.

Now, without spelling out the number: '10' itself totally explicitly...;
let's choose any pair of two numbers which when combined together do make up a total of the number: 10...
A/1: 1,9
B/2: 2,8
C/3: 3,7
D/4: 4,6
E/5: 5,5
F/6: 6,4
G/7: 7,3
H/8: 8,2
I/9: 9,1
...then, immediately, we can see that though there are, at least, 9 different ways to arrive at the number totalling: 10;
yet, it's not so easy to state precisely which 2 numbers were used to get there...?
It could be any of 9 different possible routes.

Similarly, if that number were higher in total...say, 100/or, 1000/-etc.; 
then, the amount of possible 2 number routes that could be used to get there...
would be even more.

This is a form of 'data-hiding- technique.  

Of course, it's also possible to extend this number totalling method to include say...
3 different numbers to make up the number 10:
1+3+6
...or, 4 different numbers...
2+3+1+4
...we could even choose to include the number 0, as well...
2+0+5+3
-etc.

-----

So, you could have 3 different password levels...

Level 1: 10 (or, enter a combination of digits which make up the number 10: 0+1+3+6 = 0136)
Level 2: C
Level 3: 37
